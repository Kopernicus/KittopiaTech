## v2.0.0
This release is basically a full rewrite of Kittopia.

Here's the highlights:
* The UI has been rewritten from the ground up to be much nicer to use.
* Just about everything exposed by kopernicus should now be editable.
* Compatibility with HotReload, so you can hot-reload PQSMods on the fly.
* and much more!
